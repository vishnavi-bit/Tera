# Dealer: Dealer stock view

Requirement REQ-390 · Feature FEAT-1353 · Decision DEC-425
Order: 24 of 37 · Depends on: 20 OEM: Chassis validation and dispatch shipment

## Purpose
Dealer owner sees own stock by vehicle model and by spare part, to check before selling.

## Behaviour
- Stock quantity is shown by vehicle model, variant and spare part.
- Stock can be filtered by type and searched by name.
- The dealer sees only its own stock.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Stock quantity is shown by model, variant and spare part.
2. Stock can be filtered by type and searched by name.
3. The dealer sees only its own stock.
