# OEM: Manufacturing unit setup

Requirement REQ-368 · Feature FEAT-1331 · Decision DEC-403
Order: 2 of 37 · Depends on: 1 OEM: Regions, zones and operating states

## Purpose
OEM admin creates a manufacturing unit. It is the stock source, dispatch origin and letterhead for dealers.

## Behaviour
- OEM admin creates a unit with name, address, GST, bank details, phone, city, email and latitude and longitude.
- OEM admin edits a unit; the change is visible at once.
- A unit can be viewed with its details and connected dealers.

## Acceptance criteria
1. A unit can be created with all listed fields.
2. A unit can be edited and the change is visible at once.
3. The unit view shows its details and connected dealers.
