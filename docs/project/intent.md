# Intent

## Purpose
TERA EV ERP runs the EV business end to end for the manufacturer and its dealers, from stock and orders through sales and service.

## Goals
- G1: Stock and purchase orders are tracked from the manufacturer to the dealers.
- G2: Customer sales are recorded from enquiry to invoice and payment (to confirm).
- G3: Service and warranty history is kept for each vehicle (to confirm).

## Not goals
- Customer-facing public site (no public marketing or e-commerce pages).

## Success
- Every vehicle (chassis) can be traced from production to the customer sale.
- Stock can be checked before every order and sale (to confirm).
