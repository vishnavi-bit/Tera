# Business Problem & Solution

## The Business Problem

## Who has the problem
Aspiring entrepreneurs in India who want to start a business but are stuck at "I don't know where to start."

| Persona | Landing route | Situation |
|---|---|---|
| Women entrepreneurs | `/for/women-entrepreneurs` | Thinking of starting a business, often home-based |
| Working professionals | `/for/working-professionals` | Employed; exploring a side business or career switch |
| College grads | `/for/college-grads` | Want to start something instead of job-hunting |

## What hurts them
- No clear picture of **where they stand** (is my idea even viable? am I ready?)
- Generic advice that ignores their **budget, skills and city**
- Unknown **licences, local demand and competition**
- Fear of **costly first-time mistakes**
- No concrete **next steps**, so they never start

## What the business wants
Move users along: **curiosity → clarity → commitment → paid incubation.**

> "I want to start something" → "I know what to do" → "I am doing it" → "I need guidance to grow"

The product is a low-priced, personalised, AI-generated startup plan (PDF). It earns trust, then upsells into consultation and incubation. The goal is not to sell information.

## The Solution in One Paragraph

The user answers a short guided questionnaire. The service gives them a **Startup Readiness Score (0–100)** as a free teaser, plus a locked preview of the report. They unlock the full personalised **PDF report** by paying ₹59, ₹199 or ₹2,999. Each tier adds depth. After delivery, WhatsApp and email follow-ups nudge them toward the next step: a higher tier, a mentor call, or the incubation programme.
